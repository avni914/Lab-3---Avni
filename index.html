the best movie I've watched recently is Mr. Peabody and Sherman. It was one of the kids movies released back in 2014. The movie is about a dog who adopts a child, and they both go on adventures together. The dog is a "know-it-all" who knows everything. History, Sports, Arts, etc. After adopting a child, they both build a time machine, which helps them go back to the past. This helps the child gain more knowledge and history. The child - Sherman, is a bit shy and introverted. It's his first day of school and he struggles to fit in because he gets bullied for having a dog parent. After the dog, Mr.Peabody finds out about this, he invites the bully over to hopefully create a new bond of friendship. In fear, Sherman tells the bully how he is so smart and they use the time machine which sends them back to ancient times. The movie itself is very family-friendly, fun, informative, and chaotic. You get to learn so much history and fun facts about famous people such as Mona Lisa, Da vinci, and more.
